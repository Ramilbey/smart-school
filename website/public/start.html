An AI learning platform for Uzbek schools, in Uzbek, Russian and English. Pupils get a tutor that draws and speaks and a tool that finds the earlier gap behind a wrong answer. Teachers make quizzes in seconds and run live class games. No login is needed for pupils; everything opens on a phone.O‘zbekiston maktablari uchun AI ta’lim platformasi: o‘zbek, rus va ingliz tillarida. O‘qituvchilar bir zumda test tuzadi va sinf bilan jonli o‘yinlar o‘tkazadi. O‘quvchilar uchun login kerak emas; hammasi telefonda ochiladi.

Equation gap finder: the wrong answer tells us what is missingTenglamadagi bo‘shliqni topish: xato javob nima yetishmasligini aytadi

Most mistakes in Grade 7 equations come from a skill missed in Grade 5 or 6. Solve one equation; the app matches your wrong answer to the mistake behind it, checks the skills underneath on a live map, teaches the missing one in two minutes, then gives you the same equation again. It also writes a one-line summary for the teacher.7-sinf tenglamalaridagi ko‘p xatolar 5–6-sinfda o‘tkazib yuborilgan ko‘nikmadan keladi. Bitta tenglamani yeching: ilova xato javobni uning sababi bilan solishtiradi, pastdagi ko‘nikmalarni jonli xaritada tekshiradi, yetishmaganini ikki daqiqada o‘rgatadi va o‘sha tenglamani yana beradi. O‘qituvchi uchun bir qatorli xulosa ham yozadi.

What is simulatedNimalar simulyatsiya

  • Smart Attendance: the camera head-count is a fixed sample number.
  • The demo teacher's class insights use sample pupils, marked "Demo data" on screen.
  • A server with its own AI in every school is planned; today one cloud server runs everything.
  • Aqlli davomat: kamera hisobi namunaviy son.
  • Demo o‘qituvchining sinf tahlili namunaviy o‘quvchilardan iborat va ekranda «Demo data» deb belgilangan.
  • Har bir maktabda o‘z AI’li server rejalashtirilgan; hozir hammasi bitta bulut serverda ishlaydi.

Good to knowBilish foydali

  • Switch the language at the top of each page: EN · RU · UZ.
  • The gap finder, games and homework helper are computed by code, so their answers are exact and work without AI.
  • Pupil names never reach the AI: they are replaced by codes first.
  • Har bir sahifa tepasida tilni almashtiring: EN · RU · UZ.
  • Bo‘shliqni topish, o‘yinlar va uy vazifasi yordamchisi kod bilan hisoblanadi: javoblar aniq va AI’siz ishlaydi.
  • O‘quvchi ismlari AI’ga yetib bormaydi: avval kodlarga almashtiriladi.
